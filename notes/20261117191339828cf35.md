azk predates the amble CLI, so it was aligned with what `amble new` writes: the engine moved from src/lib.rs into src/engine.rs (byte-identical to the scaffold, re-exported from lib.rs), and AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md (@AGENTS.md) and a committed .claude/skills -> ../.agents/skills symlink were added, with .gitignore ignoring the rest of /.claude. The five hand-written ambler-node/-test/-util/-walk/-spec skills were replaced by the scaffold's single `ambler` skill, which defers to `amble <verb>` and `amble help`. Because amble generates per-node Utils/SystemUtils/MockUtils and a per-walk runner, AGENTS.md has a section on how azk adapts generated code (capability traits + System, testing::Fake, impl_has!, verb-prefixed node names, `amble walk --no-runner` with dispatch in src/main.rs); keep that section, not the skill, as the place for azk-only rules. The scaffold's tokio `time` feature was deliberately not re-added.
